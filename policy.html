STORE INFORMATION

Store Policies

Shipping

Delivery availability and charges are shown or confirmed during order processing. Any delivery estimate provided by X STORE is subject to the destination and courier service.

Returns

X STORE accepts return requests within 7 calendar days from the delivery date for items that arrive damaged, defective, or different from the item ordered.

  • Submit your return request within 7 calendar days after delivery.
  • Choose the applicable reason and provide a clear description of the issue.
  • Photos may be requested to help review the issue.
  • Requests are reviewed by X STORE. A request is not approved automatically.
  • Change-of-mind returns are not included in this policy.

For an approved return involving a damaged, defective, or wrong item, X STORE will cover the return shipping arrangement. The store team will provide the collection or return instructions.

Refunds

Refunds are processed after the returned item is received and inspected. Refunds are made to the original payment method where available. For Cash on Delivery orders, X STORE will confirm an appropriate verified refund method with the customer before processing.

A refund is considered complete only after the payment has actually been sent and confirmed. Processing time may depend on the payment provider or bank.

Need help?

Use the Customer Support section on the website to contact X STORE about an order or return.

Privacy

Order and return information is used to process purchases, review return requests, and provide customer support. Do not submit unnecessary sensitive information or payment-card details.

Terms

Orders may be confirmed by phone before dispatch. Product availability, prices, delivery coverage, and Cash on Delivery availability are subject to confirmation by X STORE.
