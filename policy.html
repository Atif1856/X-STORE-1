Policies

Shipping

Delivery availability, charges, and estimated delivery times will be confirmed by X STORE. The store administrator should publish accurate delivery terms before accepting live orders.

Returns and refunds

Contact X STORE to request a return or report an issue. The store owner must set and publish the final return and refund policy before launch.

Privacy

Order information is used to process and deliver purchases. Do not submit unnecessary sensitive information. The store owner must publish a complete privacy policy appropriate to the business and applicable law.

Terms

Orders may be confirmed by phone before dispatch. Prices, stock, delivery coverage, and COD availability should be verified by the store.
